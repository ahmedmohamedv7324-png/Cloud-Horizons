About Cloud Horizons

Cloud Horizons Research & Studies Center is a specialized institution dedicated to advancing geospatial technologies and environmental research. Our team of experts combines scientific rigor with innovative approaches to deliver impactful solutions.

We bridge the gap between academic research and practical applications, helping organizations leverage GIS, remote sensing, and AI technologies for sustainable development.

Vision & Mission

Our Vision

To be a leading regional center of excellence in geospatial research and environmental studies, empowering communities with data-driven insights and innovative solutions that contribute to sustainable development and informed decision-making across the Arab world.

🎯

Our Mission

To advance the fields of GIS, remote sensing, and environmental sciences through high-quality research, professional training, and practical applications. We aim to foster knowledge, build capacity, and deliver solutions that address real-world challenges in urban planning, environmental management, and technological innovation.
